CATEGORY BOUNDARY RULES (read before placing any obligation):

"Financial & Cost Allocation Systems" — ONLY includes rent mechanics, tax pass-throughs during
the active lease term, operating expense charges, and rent-based remedies (deficiency, offset).
NEVER use this category for: insurance, indemnification, broker fees, or purchase/closing costs.

"Insurance & Risk Management" — ONLY for obligations to obtain, maintain, or pay for insurance
policies (property, liability, casualty). Use this even if the obligation is in a section labelled
"Financial" in the document.

"Legal & Indemnification" — ONLY for indemnify/hold-harmless clauses, broker commission claims,
attorney fee obligations, and warranty/guaranty duties. Do NOT place these under Financial.

"Purchase Option & Closing Costs" — ONLY for obligations arising from an option-to-purchase clause:
purchase price, closing deliverables, title transfer, deed execution, lien releases, surveys,
due diligence costs, and transfer taxes. These are NOT lease financial obligations.

"Compliance & Regulatory" — Environmental obligations, hazardous materials, building code
compliance. Do NOT use for insurance (use Insurance & Risk Management).
